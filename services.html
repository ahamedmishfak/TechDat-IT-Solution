Service 02

Network engineering

End-to-end network design, deployment and ongoing management — from a ten-person office to a multi-site enterprise. We architect the solution, then install and document it so somebody else could maintain it.

From LKR 110,000 per project

LAN & WAN design

Structured cabling, switch configuration, VLAN segmentation and router setup for reliable, segmented networks.

Wi-Fi deployment

Site surveys, access point placement, guest VLAN isolation, bandwidth management and captive portals.

Firewall & VPN

Perimeter firewall configuration, site-to-site and remote-access VPN, and policy rules that match how your team works.

Server infrastructure

Windows Server, Active Directory, file shares, domain policy and automated backup for small and mid-size businesses.

CCTV & access control

IP camera systems, NVR setup, remote monitoring and door access control across single or multiple sites.

Documentation & handover

Every deployment ships with a network diagram, IP schedule and credentials handover. No black boxes.
