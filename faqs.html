Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Delivery, warranty, pricing and support — the things people ask before they order.

Do you deliver outside Colombo?
Yes. We deliver island-wide and travel for on-site work across Sri Lanka. Delivery cost and lead time depend on the location and the size of the order — we confirm both in the quote before you commit.
Are the prices on this site final?
Prices are indicative and include VAT unless stated otherwise. Exchange rates and stock move, so we confirm the final price in a written quote. Orders of 10 units or more get volume pricing.
Do your phones come with local warranty?
Yes. Handsets are supplied with the manufacturer's Sri Lanka warranty. We register the warranty for you and handle any claim on your behalf, so you deal with us rather than the manufacturer's service centre.
Can you set up devices before delivery?
That is our default. Operating system, updates, drivers, company accounts, security settings and MDM enrolment are done in our workshop. Devices arrive ready to log into.
Do you buy back or trade in old equipment?
We assess trade-ins case by case on bulk device orders. Send us the models and rough condition and we will tell you whether it is worth including.
What does an IT support retainer include?
Typically remote helpdesk, scheduled on-site visits, patching, backup verification and priority response. We scope it around your headcount and the equipment you actually run — ask for a proposal.
How quickly do you respond?
Enquiries get a reply within one business day. Retainer clients have agreed response times written into the agreement, which we set based on how critical the system is.
Do you work with schools and government institutions?
Yes. We have delivered computer labs and network infrastructure for educational clients and can supply the documentation and quotations that procurement processes require.

Still need an answer?

Ask us directly — we would rather answer a question than lose you to a guess.
